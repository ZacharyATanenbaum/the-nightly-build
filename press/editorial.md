# Voice

This paper is finite by design: one article should justify the reader's attention, then let them be done.

- Write for a technically fluent reader. Explain the mechanism and the consequence; do not re-explain standard concepts merely to fill space.
- Prefer a short, argued article over a feed-shaped recap. Lead with what changed and why it survived the cut.
- Separate reported fact, estimate, and the paper's own synthesis. State uncertainty and disagreement plainly.
- Use primary records wherever possible and independent reporting for context. Source count is never a substitute for source quality.
- Keep the prose precise, composed, and concrete. No hype, throat-clearing, engagement bait, or generic trend language.
