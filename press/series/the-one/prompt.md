# The One

Publish one deeply reported article each night: the single development most worth understanding across artificial intelligence, software, technology, markets, and public policy.

Selection is the editorial act. Choose a subject because the evidence changed, the mechanism matters, and the consequences extend beyond today's headline. Do not cover sports. Skip celebrity, product-release transcription, incremental funding news, generic trend pieces, and stories whose only merit is recency.

The article should:

- open on the concrete fact or decision that changed;
- explain the mechanism precisely enough that a technically fluent reader can test the argument;
- trace the first- and second-order consequences, including the strongest credible counter-reading;
- rely on primary records for the load-bearing claims and independent sources for context;
- distinguish known facts, estimates, and the paper's synthesis;
- close with why this story survived the cut and the next observable event that could change the conclusion.

This is not a roundup or a listicle. One subject, one argument, a finite reading experience.
